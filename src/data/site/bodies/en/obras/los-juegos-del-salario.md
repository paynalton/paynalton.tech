There are two types of workers: those who are happy in their work and give their maximum effort, and those who just wait for the clock hands to come together to shout "Yaba-daba-do" and run out the door.<br><br>The similarity between both workers is fatigue. Both will be tired at the end of the day, but the first will feel that their effort was worth it while the second will feel they have been terribly exploited.<br><br>The next day, one will work harder and the other will try to work less.<br><br>The difference between both workers can be made by each person individually, but it is more effective if the change occurs in their work activities.
