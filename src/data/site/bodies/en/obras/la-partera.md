The Teserita glared at the doctor, but upon seeing his feathers dyed with the colors of genuine despair, he understood there was truly nothing that could be done and forced himself to remain calm.

Looking desolately in all directions, searching for something, a small hope, a miracle that could save his wife who was about to give birth prematurely far from any healer for live births. Everyone around him kept silent and avoided looking at him, since they had surely heard the whole conversation.

Still agitated, one of them spoke for the group, apparently translating his words. None of them seemed to react or calm down. The human walked very slowly, leaning on the staff as if it were a third leg. He got next to his wife and gently pierced her with his mating spines while she pushed at the rhythm the human indicated.

Everything went as if it were a dream. With the most terrible scream that she had ever heard, her wife expelled the birth sac. In a hospital, he would have entered smoothly into a neonatal capsule where an automatic system would have done everything necessary for his son to be born, but here...

The human received the sac with her own hands and, sinking her fingers, broke the membrane to pull out the neonate. With incredible skill, she cut the three cords and tied them with a thread that had been soaked in alcohol, cleaned all the fluids with a cloth soaked in warm water, and cleared his airways by breathing with her own mouth and spitting into a pail. The doctor hurriedly exited the room and in the distance, he could be heard vomiting. It must have been his first viviparous birth, at least without neonatal capsules. But the human seemed to have done this many times, the other humans looked at her with admiration, and now he did too.

When she finished, she wrapped the new being in a cloth so tightly that he could only move his head and eyes, took him to them, and delivered him as if he were a gift.
