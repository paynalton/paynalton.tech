There exists, beyond time, a place called eternity. The creatures that inhabit this place are strange and mysterious, as well as innocent and brilliant.<br><br>They say that from time to time one of these creatures gets lost on the way, finding themselves suddenly surrounded by chaos and confusion. They also say that these creatures live a long time immersed in the fear and pain caused by confusion. They are seen fighting against a world that rejects them, painfully facing situations they cannot understand or control.<br><br>They say that sometimes these creatures find the answer to their worries, realize that by accident they abandoned eternity, and it is then that they return to where they belong.
